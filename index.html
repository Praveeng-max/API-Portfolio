How Driven manages it

The work behind the figures

Leasing

Every vacancy is marketed, viewed and negotiated by our leasing team, with offers reported back to you before acceptance. 193 units are on the market today.

Renewals

Notices are issued 90 days before expiry and rents reviewed against the RERA index, so income continues without a gap. 337 leases are in hand for the next quarter.

Collection

Cheques are logged, banked and tracked per unit, with any shortfall followed up immediately and reported to you.

Compliance

Ejari registration, DEWA transfers and move-in and move-out inspections are handled for every tenancy.

Maintenance

Requests are logged, quoted and approved by you before work starts, with photographs on completion.

Reporting

You receive this review with every position update, so the portfolio is never a black box between statements.
